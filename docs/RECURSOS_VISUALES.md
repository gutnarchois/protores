# Recursos visuales de Protores 0.3.0

Creados con la herramienta integrada `imagegen`. El logo es propio de Protores; no representa un escudo oficial de la institución. Los PNG originales generados se conservaron y se copiaron al proyecto.

- Logo con transparencia: `mobile/assets/images/protores-logo.png`.
- Ilustración del restaurante: `mobile/assets/images/restaurante-escolar.png`.
- Copias para la página de descarga: `web/dist/assets/protores-logo.png` y `web/dist/assets/restaurante-escolar.png`.
- Iconos Android: generados desde el logo con `dart run flutter_launcher_icons`; configuración en `mobile/pubspec.yaml`. Incluye versiones por densidad y un icono adaptativo.

## Prompt del logo

Use case: logo-brand. Asset type: production logo and Android app icon for Protores, a Colombian school dining turn-management application. Create one polished, simple, highly legible flat emblem: a rounded green plate forming a clock, with a white fork and spoon integrated into its left and right sides, and two navy clock hands in the center. Friendly, contemporary school service identity. Palette emerald green #15995d, dark navy #0a2350, white; a tiny warm orange accent is optional. Centered square composition, bold clean geometric shapes, generous safe padding for Android launcher cropping, crisp edges, no shadows, no mockup, no surrounding objects. No letters, no words, no lettering. Transparent background. Deliver the actual usable bitmap mark.

## Prompt de la ilustración

Use case: illustration-story. Asset type: wide welcome illustration embedded in Protores, a school dining app in Colombia. Create a polished friendly editorial illustration of an orderly school cafeteria: three school students carrying lunch trays with balanced meals, smiling cafeteria staff serving, a clean bright lunch counter, a simple wall clock and a few green plants. Modern flat illustration with softly rounded geometric shapes and restrained details. Emerald green #15995d, navy #0a2350, warm orange, cream and pale blue. Landscape wide composition, all subjects comfortably inside the frame with generous breathing room, soft pale blue background. Optimistic, calm, welcoming. No letters, no text, no logos, no watermarks. Production-ready illustration, not a phone mockup, no UI frames.
